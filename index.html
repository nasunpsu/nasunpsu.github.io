Hi, I'm Na.

I'm a UX researcher who works the full stack of a platform: the AI surface people touch, the admin console that governs it, and the APIs other companies build on. Seven years at Google Workspace, a PhD in HCI from Penn State, and most of my work starts before there's a product to test.

🎉 Just launched: Google Pics is live — object-based AI image editing inside Workspace, where you point at the thing you want changed instead of describing it. Announced September 1, 2026, across Workspace Business and Enterprise, Education, and the consumer AI tiers.

I led the UX research on it from 0→1: the foundational work before there was a prototype, the studies that shaped the editing interaction model, and the readiness research that preceded launch. How that went →
