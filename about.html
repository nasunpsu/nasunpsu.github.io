Hi, I'm Na Sun.

Senior UX Researcher, Google Workspace

I've spent the last seven years inside Google Workspace doing research that sits right at the point where a product decision either builds trust or spends it — generative AI tools learning what they're allowed to touch, permission systems deciding who gets access to what, platforms negotiating between end-users, admins, and third-party developers.

Before Google, I was a PhD student at Penn State's College of Information Sciences and Technology, where I spent six years building and validating the kind of quasi-experimental, psychometric research infrastructure that most industry researchers never get the runway to build from scratch. That rigor is still the backbone of how I work — I just aim it at product roadmaps now instead of dissertation chapters.

I'm currently exploring senior IC, research lead, and PM-adjacent roles at smaller, AI-native companies — I want to be closer to the build again, and I'm putting real hours into sharpening my own technical range (this site included) alongside the research craft.

How I got here

2019 — Present

Senior UX Researcher, Google Workspace & Core Data

0-to-1 generative AI product research, enterprise access governance, and platform/ecosystem research across Chat, Drive, and the Admin Console. Shaped a multi-quarter platform roadmap across 20+ PMs and scaled the platform's developer-satisfaction (HaTS) program by 560%.

2013 — 2019

PhD, Human-Computer Interaction — Penn State

Built and validated a 5-factor psychometric framework via EFA/CFA on a large-scale survey (N = 740), triangulating survey data with behavioral logs via ANCOVA/regression. Built full-stack prototypes (a research chatbot and web app) to study digital community dynamics — the throughline from "researcher" to "researcher who ships things" starts here.
