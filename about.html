About

Senior UX Researcher · Google · PhD, Information Technology (HCI focus)

As a Senior UX Researcher at Google, I currently work on Google Pics, a newly announced AI-powered image creation and editing tool for Workspace, focused on enabling visual creativity for everyday users. Before that, I led foundational research on access governance and external collaboration (including Guest Pass) for the Identity and Groups Platform, UX research for the Chat Platform (helping developers build better Workspace chat app integrations), and UX research for Core Data across crawling, processing, indexing, and analytics products.

With over seven years of UX research experience, I have a strong background in human-computer interaction (HCI) and computer-supported cooperative work (CSCW), and a PhD in Information Technology from Penn State with a focus on HCI. My core competencies are designing and conducting mixed-methods research, developing and evaluating chatbots, and leading co-design workshops and research programs — applied across app development, data infrastructure, social computing, sense-making, and online education. My research has informed multiple successful product launches, measurable gains in user satisfaction, and publications in top-tier conferences.

My mission is to create user-centered, data-driven solutions that connect people and empower communities. Alongside that, I've been deepening my quantitative and business-strategy fluency — a natural extension of research that's already shaping strategic product decisions.
