Résumé

Senior UX Researcher · PhD, Information Technology (HCI focus) · Google, 2019–Present

Senior UX Researcher with a PhD in HCI and a computer science background, working the full stack of a platform: the 0→1 generative AI surface people touch, the admin console that governs it, and the APIs and agentic workflows developers build on. My research spans how knowledge workers judge the value AI actually delivers, how people come to trust conversational agents, and how IT admins reason about identity and group permissions — mixed methods throughout, with enough technical depth to design studies alongside engineers rather than around them. Alongside my own studies I build the practice around the work: leading my team's research critique sessions, running an academic speaker series that connects university researchers with product teams, and mentoring researchers and doctoral students.
